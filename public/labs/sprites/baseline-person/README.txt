# Baseline person

Ten transparent 32 x 32 PNGs. Upload each file to its matching Character sprites slot in the Custom Character lab.

front.png = Front
left.png = Left idle
left1.png = Left walk 1
left2.png = Left walk 2
right.png = Right idle
right1.png = Right walk 1
right2.png = Right walk 2
jump.png = Jump
crouch.png = Crouch
faint.png = Faint

sprite-sheet.png: 160 x 64 atlas, five columns by two rows, in the order above.
preview.png: enlarged preview only; do not upload it as a pose.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then sliced and resized with nearest-neighbor sampling to preserve hard pixel edges and transparency.
Prompt: A consistent basic pixel-art person with dark brown skin, short black hair, a plain green shirt, blue pants, and dark shoes. Transparent background, limited flat palette, all ten poses arranged in five columns and two rows: front, left idle, left walk 1, left walk 2, right idle; right walk 1, right walk 2, jump, crouch, faint. No labels or grid lines.
