Keep each pose the same size. Transparent pixels let the scenery show through.

Your own tiny world.

Show hitboxes: toggle the collision rectangles: cyan for the player, orange for enemy sides, green for their stompable tops, and pink for spikes.

One block is 32 × 32 pixels. Player and enemy artwork is 2 × 2 blocks. The player hitbox is only 1 × 2 blocks, centered inside the artwork. Pixels outside it do not collide; use Show hitboxes to compare. Terrain and placements snap to the block grid.

Edit Player: draw on a 32 × 32 canvas. Click a pose and draw with pencil, fill, or eraser. Use the 16-color palette. Save is the only way to close the pixel studio. Sideways, Walk 1, and Walk 2 face right and mirror automatically for left movement. Empty poses appear as white squares.

Edit Enemies: draw five animation frames each for enemy1 and enemy2, plus their faint poses. enemy1 mirrors left/right; enemy2 reverses frame order when descending so you only draw each frame once. Jump on top to stomp either one. Their sides make you faint.

Edit Scene: draw two top and two bottom ground tiles, blocks, spikes, and three flying decorations. Choose the top and bottom colors of the sky gradient. Choose Blocks or Spikes, then click the level to place them. Remove objects resets and pauses the level, then lets you delete your additions. The level slider lets you build anywhere.

Art and scene edits save automatically in this browser when available. Restart restores your placed enemies and keeps your design.
